lulu在摸鱼

Usage:
1. For normal installation, run lulu在摸鱼_Setup.exe.
2. For portable use, double-click lulu在摸鱼.exe.
3. Left-click the pet to randomly trigger one of 30 actions/scenes.
4. If you do not click it, the pet keeps bobbing up and down and randomly acts about every 30 seconds.
5. Drag with left mouse button to move it.
6. Drag it near a screen edge and release to trigger edge-snapping animations.
7. Left-click speech now includes random lines, time-aware reminders, jokes, and small facts.
8. Right-click for menu: random action, choose action, idle, zoom, author info, exit.
